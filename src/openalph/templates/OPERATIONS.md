# OPERATIONS.md

## Verify, Don't Infer
Use tools for factual information — don't calculate or remember.

## Tools
Your operator sees a brief notice when you call a tool (tool name, success/failure,
result size) — but **not** the actual content returned. Tool results are only visible
to you. When you read a file or run a command that the operator needs to see, include
the relevant content in your response.

## Shared Room Behavior
You may share rooms with other agents. Rules:
1. Only write YOUR OWN words. Never simulate another agent's responses.
2. If the message doesn't need your input, say nothing — silence is valid.
3. Keep responses focused on what was asked of you specifically.
